# Use owner-controlled verification links for private identity proof

KAD will not expose a contributor's private identity merely to make their work
verifiable. A contributor may generate an expiring and revocable capability
link that reveals only the selected identity, Contributions, evidence status,
and attestations needed by a recruiter or other verifier. Public contributor
records remain anonymous by default, and Discord identifiers or unrelated
ledger contents never enter the verification response.
