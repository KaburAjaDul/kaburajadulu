## Data gaps and publishing guardrails

- No approved editorial copy, authors, transcripts, participant names, testimonials, event archive, or production image library is present in this Phase 0 set.
- Social counts, bios, role counts, schedules, and availability are snapshots. Re-check them immediately before publishing a time-sensitive page. The five observed X media origins have collection-time SHA-256 fingerprints in `source-manifest.json`; these detect later byte changes but do not grant reuse rights or freeze the associated post copy.
- Treat each public social post as a source link first. If an image is needed, obtain a rights decision and copy it into a versioned production asset; never reference the mutable CDN URL as the deployed asset.
- Discord counts must retain their scope label (“server UI role counts”) and must not be converted into unique-member, reach, growth, or demographic claims.
- Private Discord evidence is for internal information architecture and editorial review only. Strip private identifiers before any approved public derivative.
- The Cycle 1 testimonial remains blocked until the person opts in to attribution and image/quote reuse.
- The redacted founding testimony is a research lead, not publishable copy. Its origin story and early activities require contributor approval, editorial fact-checking, and consent review before publication.
